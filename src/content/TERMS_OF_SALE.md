## 1. Registration and payment method

You must be 18 or older and provide accurate registration information.

To bid, you must add a valid credit or debit card. When you add a card we place
a **$1.00 authorization hold** to confirm it is valid. This hold is released
immediately and no money is taken. If the card cannot be authorized, you will
not be able to bid until a working card is provided.

Your card details are held by our payment processor, Stripe. We never see or
store your full card number.

Accounts may require approval before bidding. We may decline or revoke an
account at our discretion.

## 2. Bidding

**Lots open at $0.00** unless otherwise stated. Some lots carry a **reserve** —
a minimum price below which the lot will not sell. Reserves are not disclosed.

**Bidding is by proxy (maximum bid).** You enter the most you are willing to
pay. The system bids on your behalf only as much as needed to keep you in the
lead, up to your maximum. Bid increments scale with price.

**Soft close (anti-snipe).** If a bid is placed in the final minutes of a lot's
closing window, that lot's closing time extends. A lot does not close until
bidding on it has stopped.

**Every bid is binding.** A winning bid is a contract to buy. Bids cannot be
retracted.

Each lot closes at its own scheduled time. Lots within one auction may close at
staggered times.

## 3. What you pay

Your total for each lot is:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hammer price** | your winning bid |
| **+ Buyer's premium** | a percentage of the hammer price, stated on every auction |
| **+ Sales tax** | where applicable (see section 7) |

The buyer's premium is currently **15%** unless a specific auction states
otherwise. The rate in effect for an auction is shown before you bid and on
every invoice.

Hammer price and buyer's premium are always shown as separate lines. We will
never show you a total without showing what it is made of.

## 4. Payment

**Your card is charged automatically when a lot closes and you are the winning
bidder.** You do not need to do anything. You will receive an email confirming
the lot, hammer price, buyer's premium and total charged.

**If your card is declined**, we will email you. You are still obligated to pay.
We may retry the card, request another payment method, and may cancel the sale
and re-offer the lot if payment cannot be completed. Repeated payment failures
may result in account suspension.

## 5. Collection and shipping

At the close of each auction you choose **local pickup** or **shipping**.

### Local pickup

Pickup is at [PICKUP ADDRESS] during the pickup window stated on the auction.
**The pickup window is 3 days from the close of the auction**, with exact dates
and times shown on the auction page before you bid and repeated in your
winning-bid email.

If you cannot collect within the window, contact us **before it closes** and we
will arrange shipping at your cost, provided the item is shippable. We would far
rather ship you your lot than have anyone lose it.

**Lots not collected within the pickup window, and for which no shipping
arrangement has been made, are forfeited.** Forfeited lots may be resold or
disposed of, and no refund is due. We will send at least one reminder before the
window closes.

We understand this is strict. It exists because we do not have storage, and it
is disclosed before you bid so you can decide accordingly. If you are unsure you
can collect in time, choose shipping.

### Shipping

Shipping is by USPS with tracking, at the buyer's cost. Tracking details are
emailed when the label is created. Risk of loss passes to the buyer on delivery
to the carrier. We are not responsible for carrier delays.

We ship within the United States only.

We photograph each lot and describe it in good faith, including visible flaws.
Descriptions are our opinion, not a warranty. Photographs form part of the
description — please examine them.

**All sales are final**, with one exception:

> **If a lot is materially not as described, we will make it right.** Contact us
> within **3 days** of receiving or collecting the lot, with photographs. If we
> agree the lot is significantly different from its description, we will refund
> the hammer price and buyer's premium in full, including return shipping where
> applicable.

"Materially not as described" means a substantive difference — wrong item,
undisclosed major damage, misidentified maker. It does not mean a change of
mind, a lot being smaller than imagined, or normal wear that was disclosed or
visible in photographs.

We do not offer returns for any other reason.
