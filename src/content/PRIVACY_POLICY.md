# Privacy Policy: What The Find

**Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]**

This policy explains what we collect when you use whatthefind.live, why, who helps us run it, and the choices you
have. "We" means [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], doing business as What The Find. Questions: **whatthefind.co@gmail.com**.

The short version: we collect what we need to run auctions, take payment and get your lots to you. We don't sell
your information, we don't run advertising trackers, and you can delete your account at any time.

---

## 1. What we collect

**When you create an account**
- Username, email address and password (stored only as a one-way scrambled form; we can't read it).

**When you complete your profile to bid**
- Full name, phone number and address (for shipping, pickup and contacting you about orders).
- A payment card. **Your card is held by Stripe, our payment processor; we never see or store the full number.**
  We keep only Stripe's reference to it and whether the $1 check passed.
- Your shipping or pickup choice for each auction.

**When you bid and buy**
- Your bids, maximum bids and pre-bids, the lots you win, your orders, invoices, payments, refunds and shipping
  details (including tracking).
- Rewards points and promo codes you use, if rewards are switched on.

**When you use other features**
- **Watch list and followed auctions:** the lots you watch, the auctions you follow, and your reminder settings.
- **Live shows:** the chat messages you send. In live shows your **username** appears next to your bids, wins and
  chat messages for everyone watching. (In timed auctions, nobody else sees who is bidding.) We don't use viewers'
  cameras or microphones; only the host broadcasts.
- **Phone alerts:** if you turn them on, your browser gives us a technical address to send alerts to. It contains
  nothing about you beyond that.
- **Services form (Sell to us, Consign, Estates, Collection auctions, Contact):** your name, email, phone, city or
  ZIP, what you tell us about your items, how many, how you heard about us, and any photos you attach.

**Automatically**
- Your login is kept in your browser's storage so you stay signed in. We don't use advertising or analytics
  cookies. Stripe may set its own cookies on the card form to prevent fraud.
- Like every website, our servers see your IP address and browser type when you visit. We use them to keep the
  site running and to stop abuse (for example, limiting repeated attempts), not to profile you.

## 2. How we use it

- To run auctions: record bids, decide winners, show you your status.
- To take payment: check your card, charge it when you win, handle declines and refunds.
- To deliver: pack, ship (we share your name, address and phone with the carrier) or arrange pickup.
- To email you: things you need (receipts, winning-bid and payment emails, declines, shipping, password resets) and
  things you chose (outbid notices, watch-list and auction reminders, "show starting now" alerts). Every optional
  email has a one-click way to stop it, and your profile page has all the switches.
- To answer service requests.
- To keep the site safe and fair: approving accounts, stopping fraud and abuse, enforcing our Terms of Sale.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

## 4. Who can see what

- **Other users:** in timed auctions, only the current price and the number of bids. In live shows, your username
  on bids, wins and chat. Never your name, email, address, phone or maximum bid.
- **Our staff:** what's needed to run the business. Service-request photos are stored privately and seen only by us.
- Our pickup address is given only to winners who chose pickup.

## 5. How long we keep it

- **Account and profile:** while your account is open.
- **Orders, invoices and payment records:** as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after an account is
  deleted.
- **Service requests:** until the request is closed and no longer needed, or sooner if you ask; deleting a request
  deletes its photos.
- **Live show chat:** kept with the show's record; a message the host deletes is hidden from everyone.

## 6. Your choices

- **Change your details** on your profile page.
- **Turn off optional emails and alerts** with the link in any of them or on your profile page.
- **Delete your account** from your profile page. We remove your name, email, phone, address and card reference,
  your watch list, follows, reminders, points and phone alerts, and replace your username with an anonymous one on
  past bids and orders. Records we must keep for tax and accounting stay, without your contact details. You can't
  delete an account while you're leading or holding a max bid on a lot that's still open, have an unpaid order, or
  have an order not yet shipped or collected; the profile page tells you which.
- **Ask us** for a copy of your information or to correct or delete something: email whatthefind.co@gmail.com.

## 7. Security

Passwords are scrambled, cards stay with Stripe, the database only answers our own server, photos you send us are
private, and access is limited to what each part needs. No system is perfect; if something happens that affects
your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

## 8. Age

The site is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account.
