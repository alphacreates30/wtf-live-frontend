### Live shows

A live show is an auction you watch as it happens. We hold each piece up to the camera, talk about it, and
it sells on a short countdown. Bid from your phone or computer.

**Before the show**
- Shows are listed on the homepage under Coming next. Tap **Remind me** and we'll email you (and send a phone alert,
  if you've turned them on) the moment we go live.
- You can **pre-bid**: set the most you'd pay for a lot before the show starts. When that lot comes up, we bid for
  you, only as much as needed, up to your limit.
- If a show doesn't start within 24 hours of its scheduled time, your pre-bids are released and we email you.
  Nothing is charged.

**Watching**
- Anyone can watch. To bid, you need an approved account and a card on file (we check it with a $1 hold that's
  released straight away).
- On iPhone, tap the picture once if there's no sound.

**Bidding**
- Each lot opens at its starting price and runs on a countdown: usually 60, 30, 15 or 5 seconds.
- Tap **Bid** to bid the next amount shown, or set a **max bid** and we'll bid for you up to it.
- **A bid in the last 5 seconds adds 5 seconds**, so everyone gets a fair last chance. Some lots are marked
  **Sudden death**: no extra time, the lot ends when the clock hits zero.
- If two people's max bids are the same, the one who reached that amount first leads.
- In live shows, **your username shows** next to your bids, your wins and your chat messages. Your max bid never
  shows, and neither does anything else about you.

**Winning and paying**
- When the clock runs out, the highest bid wins and **your card is charged for that lot right away**.
- **There's no buyer's premium in live shows**, and no hidden reserve. Your winning bid is the price (plus sales tax
  where it applies).
- Every bid is binding.

**If your card is declined**
- We'll tell you in the show and by email. Until you update your card, you can't bid in that show.
- Update it on your profile page and we'll charge it straight away.
- If it isn't sorted, we may offer that lot again, in the same show or later. If we do, you won't be charged for it,
  even if you update your card afterwards.

**Shipping**
- **We combine everything you win in one show into as few boxes as possible.** Most orders go in one box; a big or
  awkward haul may need more than one.
- Postage is charged after packing, at the actual cost of each box; we don't add a handling fee. If your wins need
  more than one box, you'll see each one's postage and tracking by email.
- Pickup, where offered, is shown on the show page.

**After the show**
- You'll get one email listing everything you won and what was charged.

**Chat**
- Be kind. Links, email addresses and phone numbers aren't allowed in chat, and neither is offensive language.
- The host may slow chat down, remove messages, or mute or remove someone from a show.
- We sometimes run **giveaways and special deals** during a show. The rules for each one are announced in the show,
  and no purchase is needed to enter a giveaway.
- Please don't arrange buying or selling with other viewers in chat; everything we sell goes through the site.
